协同效应：哪些是真的，有多大Synergies: which are real, and how big

  • 一套算力，多处复用：同一批TPU与数据中心同时服务搜索、YouTube、Gemini和云客户。外部需求强时卖给云客户，内部AI需求强时自用，这种"可互换的产能"降低了资本开支判断失误的代价。One fleet, many uses: the same TPUs and data centers serve Search, YouTube, Gemini and Cloud customers. When outside demand is strong the capacity is sold; when internal AI demand rises it is used in-house. That fungibility lowers the cost of getting capex wrong.
  • 一个模型，多个十亿级产品：Gemini的训练成本被分摊到搜索（AI Overviews月活超25亿、AI Mode超10亿）、Gemini App（9.5亿）、安卓、Workspace和苹果新Siri。对手的同等模型需要自己的分发去摊薄成本。One model, several billion-user products: Gemini's training cost is spread across Search (AI Overviews above 2.5B monthly users, AI Mode above 1B), the Gemini app (950M), Android, Workspace and Apple's new Siri. Rivals must build their own distribution to amortize a comparable model.
  • 广告系统直接接住新流量：AI Mode里的广告沿用原有的广告主、出价与衡量体系（AI Max已有超过50万广告主），新界面上线即可变现，不需要重新积累客户。The ad system catches the new traffic: ads in AI Mode reuse the existing advertisers, bidding and measurement (AI Max already has 500,000+ advertisers), so a new surface monetizes on day one.
  • 云把自用技术变成对外产品：TPU、Gemini、安全（Mandiant、Wiz）与BigQuery被打包卖给企业，收入反过来分摊基础设施成本。Cloud turns internal technology into products: TPUs, Gemini, security (Mandiant, Wiz) and BigQuery are packaged for enterprises, and the revenue in turn spreads the infrastructure cost.
  • 订阅捆绑降低流失：Google One把存储、Gemini高级功能和YouTube Premium Lite打包，单个用户同时依赖多项服务，更难离开。Bundles lower churn: Google One combines storage, advanced Gemini and YouTube Premium Lite, so one user depends on several services and is harder to lose.
第二增长曲线已经出现The second growth curve is already here

云业务Q2'26年化收入约990亿美元、增长82%，2026年上半年它对集团收入增长的贡献（约10个百分点）已与搜索相当。TPU系统销售从2026年Q2开始确认、主要在2027年放量，可能成为第三条曲线。所以答案是：全栈确实能产生第二增长点，并且在"基础设施"和"企业AI"两个方向上拓宽了护城河；但它同时让护城河更"贵"——回报率取决于这些投入最终的利用率与定价。Cloud ran at a ~$99B annualized rate in Q2'26, growing 82%, and in H1 2026 contributed about as much group revenue growth (~10 points) as Search. TPU system sales started in Q2 2026 and ramp mainly in 2027, a possible third curve. So yes: the stack does create a second growth engine and widens the moat in infrastructure and enterprise AI, but it also makes the moat more expensive, with returns depending on eventual utilization and pricing.

一个关键的不对称A key asymmetry

对广告业务来说，模型只要"足够好"，分发和变现系统就能赢；对云和高端订阅来说，模型必须"最好或接近最好"，因为企业和付费用户会直接比较。所以Gemini落后，对搜索广告的伤害小于对云AI解决方案与AI订阅的伤害。这也解释了为什么谷歌选择先把Flash做到极致：它服务的是十亿级免费用户的成本曲线。For advertising, a model only needs to be good enough for distribution and monetization to win; for Cloud and premium subscriptions it must be best or near-best, because enterprises and paying users compare directly. So Gemini's lag hurts search ads less than it hurts Cloud AI solutions and AI subscriptions. It also explains why Google pushed Flash first: it serves the cost curve of a billion free users.

为什么"自己发电"有战略意义Why owning power generation is strategic

AI数据中心的交付节奏越来越取决于并网与发电，而不是服务器。把发电与负载"同步建设"，可以绕开电网排队、锁定电价，也减少了对公用事业的依赖。代价是资本密集度进一步上升，并把能源项目的执行风险带进了资产负债表（未开始的租赁852亿美元、采购承诺中含照付不议的能源合同）。对你的存储研究而言，这意味着谷歌的服务器拉货节奏会越来越受电力上线时间约束。AI data-center delivery increasingly depends on grid connection and generation rather than servers. 关键结论：谷歌卖的是"决策时刻"，不是"点击"The key point: Google sells decision moments, not clicks

上表四项里，点击率在降，另外三项在升或持平。只要"有购买意图的决策时刻"还发生在谷歌的界面上（无论是传统结果页、AI Overview还是AI Mode），广告主就会按转化价值竞价，点击变少可以被单价上升抵消。移动互联网时代有过类似的一幕：移动端单次点击价格曾连续多年同比下降，市场一度担心"移动会杀死谷歌"，最终靠点击量增长和广告格式变化消化了。这一次真正的风险，是决策时刻本身被搬走。Of the four terms, click-through is falling while the other three are rising or flat. As long as decision moments with purchase intent still happen on Google's surfaces (classic results, AI Overviews or AI Mode), advertisers bid on conversion value, and fewer clicks can be offset by higher prices. The mobile era had a similar episode: mobile CPCs fell year over year for years and markets feared mobile would kill Google, yet click growth and new ad formats absorbed it. The real risk this time is the decision moment itself moving elsewhere.

  • 最大的护城河："TPU＋Gemini＋数据平台"的整体性价比。这是AWS和Azure都没有的组合：AWS有自研芯片但没有自己的前沿模型，Azure有OpenAI但芯片依赖英伟达。The biggest moat: the combined price-performance of TPUs, Gemini and the data platform. Neither rival has the same combination: AWS has its own chips but no in-house frontier model, and Azure has OpenAI but depends on Nvidia for chips.
  • 能否持续：取决于三件事：TPU的成本优势能否在8t/8i上延续；Gemini能否回到第一梯队，让"AI解决方案"而不只是"算力出租"成为增长主力；以及积压能否从少数AI实验室扩散到更广泛的企业客户。Is it durable: it depends on three things: whether the TPU cost edge carries into 8t/8i; whether Gemini returns to the top tier so that AI solutions, not just compute rental, drive growth; and whether the backlog broadens from a few AI labs to a wide enterprise base.
  • 会被怎样侵蚀：AI实验室多供应商化（Anthropic与OpenAI都在大规模采购Trainium）；英伟达新品与代工产能分配；微软凭借Microsoft 365与企业协议的"捆绑式"企业分发；甲骨文与新兴云的低价GPU算力；以及硬件销售与第三方算力对利润率的稀释。How it could erode: AI labs multi-sourcing (Anthropic and OpenAI are both buying Trainium at scale); new Nvidia parts and foundry allocation; Microsoft's bundled enterprise distribution through Microsoft 365 and enterprise agreements; cheap GPU capacity from Oracle and neoclouds; and margin dilution from hardware sales and third-party capacity.
