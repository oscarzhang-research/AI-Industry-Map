核心判断（非投资建议）Bottom line (not investment advice)

这是一份每一行都超预期、并把紧缺期明确延长到 2027–2028的财报：营收 $54.2B（指引 $50B），FQ1 指引 $61.5B；战略客户协议（SCA）从 16 份增至 26 份，剩余履约义务（RPO）约 $1,500 亿，客户财务承诺 $320 亿；2027 年 >75% 的产出已被锁定。

但有三件事解释了股价为何几乎不动：① 涨价斜率已从每季 +60% 级别降到 +10–20% 级别（价格的二阶导为负）；② 管理层承认客户在压缩单机内存增量（“内容增长低于此前预期”）；③ 全行业正在为 2028 年末以后集中建洁净室——供给之墙正在被浇筑。

剔除第 14 周后，FQ4 与 FQ1 指引的周均营收增速都在 +22% 左右，基本面没有减速；减速的是价格曲线，而股价交易的正是它。本报告把概率加权价值从 7 月的 $520 上修至约 $960（说明性测算，可在第 15 节自行调参），原因是高利润期的“久期”被证实得更长，而不是周期性消失了。

Every line beat, and the shortage window was explicitly extended into 2027–2028: revenue of $54.2B against a $50B guide, FQ1 guided to $61.5B, strategic customer agreements (SCAs) up from 16 to 26 with about $150B of remaining performance obligations (RPO) and $32B of customer commitments, and more than 75% of 2027 output already committed.

Three things explain why the stock barely moved: (1) quarterly price increases have slowed from roughly +60% to +10–20% (the second derivative of price is negative); (2) management conceded that customers are trimming memory content growth; (3) the whole industry is now pouring concrete for cleanrooms that open from late 2028 — the supply wall is being built.

Strip out the 14th week and revenue per week grew about 22% in FQ4 and is guided to grow about 22% again in FQ1. The business is not decelerating; the price curve is, and that is what the stock trades on. This report lifts its probability-weighted value from July's $520 to about $960 (illustrative; adjust it yourself in Section 15) because the high-profit window has proven longer, not because the cycle has gone away.

一页结论：十个判断Bottom line: ten findings

先给结论，再给证据。下面十条按重要性排序，每一条在后文都有对应章节、数据与原文出处。凡是“推算”“说明性”的数字，都在附录写明了算法。 Conclusions first, evidence after. The ten findings below are ranked by importance; each maps to a later section with data and primary sources. Anything labeled "derived" or "illustrative" has its method spelled out in the appendix.

  1. 超预期的幅度很大，质量也高。营收比指引中值高 $4.2B（+8.5%），比 LSEG 一致预期高 6.2%；EPS 高于指引 7.8%。FQ1 指引 $61.5B / EPS $38.15，比一致预期分别高 7.9% 和 7.8%。唯一的瑕疵是 FQ1 毛利率指引 86.25%，略低于约 86.7% 的预期——这是本轮上行以来第一次给出环比走低的毛利率指引，管理层归因于约 $10 亿的激励薪酬与新厂启动成本；剔除后约 87.9%，内在毛利仍在扩张。P2T6

    The beat was large and clean. Revenue landed $4.2B (+8.5%) above the guide midpoint and 6.2% above LSEG consensus; EPS beat the guide by 7.8%. The FQ1 guide of $61.5B / $38.15 sits 7.9% and 7.8% above consensus. The one blemish is the 86.25% FQ1 gross-margin guide, slightly below the ~86.7% expected — the first sequentially lower margin guide of this upcycle, attributed to roughly $1B of incentive pay and fab start-up cost. Excluding those, margin is about 87.9% and still expanding.

  2. “增速放缓”有一大半是日历假象。FY26 是 53 周年，FQ4 有 14 周。按周均计算，FQ4 营收环比增长 +21.5%（报告口径 +31%），FQ1 指引对应周均 +22%（报告口径仅 +13%）。基本面动量是平的，不是在减速。同一口径下 DRAM 周均出货位元是负增长（约 −2.5%）：卡住增长的是供给，不是需求。（第 3 节）

    Much of the "slowdown" is a calendar artifact. FY26 had 53 weeks, so FQ4 had 14. Per week, FQ4 revenue grew 21.5% sequentially (31% as reported), and the FQ1 guide implies about +22% per week (only +13% as reported). Momentum is flat, not fading. On the same basis DRAM bits shipped per week fell about 2.5%: supply is the binding constraint, not demand. (Section 3)

  3. 价格水平还在涨，涨价斜率在放平。美光实现的 DRAM 价格环比从 +60% 出头（FQ3）降到 +10% 后段（FQ4），FQ1 隐含约 +10%；NAND 从 +80% 中段降到约 +30%。TrendForce 预计 4Q26 合约价 DRAM +10–15%、NAND +15–20%。存储股历史上往往在涨价速度见顶时见顶，而不是在价格见顶时——MU 的股价高点（6/25）恰好落在 2Q26 涨价最快的阶段之后。（第 4、11 节）

    Price levels are still rising; the slope is flattening. Micron's realized DRAM price went from +low-60s% (FQ3) to +high-teens (FQ4), with about +10% implied for FQ1; NAND went from +mid-80s% to about +30%. TrendForce sees 4Q26 contract prices up 10–15% (DRAM) and 15–20% (NAND). Memory equities have historically topped when the rate of price increase peaks, not when prices peak — and MU's high (June 25) came right after the fastest-pricing quarter, 2Q26. (Sections 4, 11)

  4. 利润倒挂：商品型产品比“护城河”产品更赚钱。CDBU 与 MCBU 毛利率 90%，HBM 占比最高的 CMBU 只有 83%（持平，原因是“HBM 占比提高”）。HBM 的 2026 价格在一年前就谈定了，如今反而是毛利最低的 DRAM。2027 年 HBM 合约已“大幅”上调并在年初生效（TrendForce：2027 年 HBM 混合均价 +121%），这是 FY27 自 FQ2 起毛利回升的机械性来源，也是“FQ1 是全年毛利底部”的底气。（第 5、8 节）

    The margin inversion: commodity beats moat, for now. CDBU and MCBU ran at 90% gross margin; the HBM-heavy CMBU ran at 83% (flat, "higher HBM mix"). HBM's 2026 prices were set a year ago, so it is now the least profitable DRAM Micron sells. 2027 HBM contracts reset "significantly" higher in January (TrendForce: blended 2027 HBM ASP +121%) — a mechanical margin tailwind from FQ2 FY27, and the basis for calling FQ1 the margin floor. (Sections 5, 8)

  5. SCA 的“天花板”已经在数据里显形。CMBU（超大规模云厂）环比只增 18%（周均 +10%），而 CDBU +56%、AEBU +47%。既然 HBM 增速 >31%，超大规模客户的非 HBM DRAM 收入大致持平——这与最大几份协议把价格上限锁在 CQ2'26 水平完全一致。SCA 在按设计运转：用上行弹性换久期与确定性。（第 5、7 节，属推论）

    The SCA ceiling is now visible in the data. CMBU (hyperscalers) grew only 18% sequentially (+10% per week) while CDBU grew 56% and AEBU 47%. With HBM growing more than 31%, hyperscaler non-HBM DRAM revenue was roughly flat — exactly what ceilings at CQ2'26 prices on the largest agreements would produce. SCAs are working as designed: they trade upside for duration and visibility. (Sections 5, 7; inference)

  6. SCA 2.0：保底价大约是预期价格的五到六成。26 份 SCA、预计占 2030 年前营收 >35%、其中四分之三有明确定价框架（多数为上下限价格带），RPO 约 $1,500 亿（按最低价与承诺量计），客户承诺 $320 亿（多为现金）。用这些数字反推，保底价约为管理层预期价格的 50–60%；若成本不变，对应保底毛利约 70–75%，仍高于上一轮周期峰值（约 61%）。每年约 $350 亿的合同底部收入，接近 FY25 全年营收（$374 亿）。（第 7 节，可交互）

    SCA 2.0: floors sit at roughly 50–60% of expected prices. 26 SCAs, expected to be over 35% of revenue through 2030, three-quarters with a defined pricing framework (mostly floor-and-ceiling bands), about $150B of RPO at minimum prices and committed volumes, and $32B of commitments (mostly cash). Working backward, floors are roughly 50–60% of management's expected prices; at flat costs that is a floor gross margin of roughly 70–75%, still above the prior-cycle peak (~61%). About $35B a year of contractual floor revenue is close to all of FY25's revenue ($37.4B). (Section 7, interactive)

  7. 紧缺的“久期”被明确拉长。措辞从“紧张延续到 2027 之后”升级为“2027、2028 年比 2026 年明显更紧”；2027 年 >75% 产出已被承诺，配额谈判已转向 2028；“看不到供需何时恢复平衡”。行业位元增速：DRAM 2027–28 年约 20% 出头、NAND 约 20% 中段，且两年都“受供给约束”。洁净室面积是行业的硬约束。（第 12 节）

    The duration of the shortage was explicitly extended. The language moved from "tight beyond 2027" to "much tighter in calendar 2027 and 2028 than 2026"; more than 75% of 2027 output is committed; allocation talks have moved on to 2028; "no line of sight" to balance. Industry bit growth: DRAM about low-20s% and NAND about mid-20s% in 2027–28, supply-constrained both years. Cleanroom space is the hard constraint. (Section 12)

  8. “降配”被承认了。管理层原话：服务器出货高增长“由略低于此前预期的内容增速所支撑”。对应外部事实：英伟达把 Vera 的 SOCAMM 从 192GB 减半到 96GB，Rubin Ultra 在评估 8 层 HBM；内存已占高端机柜成本约 25–29%，高于英伟达约 20% 的舒适区。需求弹性已经出现；管理层把它解读为“潜在需求”。两种解读都成立：它支撑久期，也压制涨价斜率。（第 8、13 节）

    De-spec was acknowledged. In management's words, high-teens server growth is "supported by a modestly lower rate of content growth than prior expectations." Outside evidence: NVIDIA halved Vera's SOCAMM from 192GB to 96GB and is evaluating 8-high HBM for Rubin Ultra; memory is ~25–29% of a high-end rack's cost versus NVIDIA's ~20% comfort level. Demand elasticity has arrived; management calls it latent demand. Both readings hold: it supports duration and caps the price slope. (Sections 8, 13)

  9. 供给之墙正在被浇筑。FY27 资本开支“高于此前计划”，上半年约 $250 亿、下半年更高（隐含全年 >$500 亿），增量以洁净室建设为主，目标是“2028 年末及以后”的空间；三星 P5 改为每栋三座晶圆厂、SK 海力士龙仁 Y1/Y2 与 M17、长鑫上海新厂、长江存储三期同步推进。这批混凝土对应的位元在 2029 年前后集中到来——周期的终点最可能在这里被建好。（第 12 节）

    The supply wall is being poured. FY27 capex is "higher versus prior plans": about $25B in the first half and more in the second (over $50B implied), led by cleanroom construction for space "in late calendar 2028 and beyond". Samsung's P5 is moving to three fabs per building; SK hynix is building Yongin Y1/Y2 and M17; CXMT is adding a Shanghai fab and YMTC its Fab 3. The bits from this concrete arrive around 2029 — the likeliest place where this cycle's end is being built. (Section 12)

  10. 估值：市场已为“新常态”付费，但不为“峰值延续到 2028 年以后”付费。$1,065 约为本报告 FY27E EPS（$179）的 5.9 倍、一致预期（$156.5）的 6.8 倍；FCF 收益率约 12%，每股净现金 $59，12 月 9 日起加大回购。反向 DCF：在 12 倍终值倍数下，现价隐含常态化 EPS 约 $84——约为 FY25 的 10 倍、FY27 峰值的一半。情景价值：熊 $527 / 基准 $947 / 牛 $1,489，概率加权约 $960。（第 15 节，可交互）

    Valuation: the market already pays for a "new normal", not for the peak lasting past 2028. At $1,065 the stock is ~5.9x this report's FY27E EPS ($179) and 6.8x consensus ($156.5); FCF yield is ~12%, net cash $59/share, and buybacks step up from Dec 9. A reverse DCF at a 12x terminal multiple implies normalized EPS of about $84 — roughly 10x FY25 and half of the FY27 peak. Scenario values: bear $527 / base $947 / bull $1,489, about $960 probability-weighted. (Section 15, interactive)

三个容易被略过的细节Three details that are easy to skip

其一，增量利润率回落了，但原因是“分钱”，不是“价格”。FQ2→FQ3 每多 $1 营收约有 $0.98 落到营业利润；FQ3→FQ4 降到 $0.86。差额几乎全部来自运营费用环比 +$10.5 亿——管理层给“每一位全球员工”提高了激励薪酬，另有 $3 亿社区投资。增量毛利率仍有 94%。这是周期中段到后段的典型画面：利润在高位，组织开始分享成果。

First, incremental margin came down, and the cause was paying people, not pricing. From FQ2 to FQ3 roughly $0.98 of every extra revenue dollar fell to operating profit; from FQ3 to FQ4 it was $0.86. Almost the whole gap is the $1.05B sequential rise in opex: higher incentive pay "for every global team member" plus a $300M community commitment. Incremental gross margin was still 94%. This is the familiar mid-to-late-cycle picture: profits at a high, and the organization starts sharing them.

其二，GAAP 里有一笔 $5 亿“专利许可费用”，对手方未披露，两场电话会都没有提。同期还有德国法院判美光侵犯长江存储两项 NAND 专利（9/23，可上诉）以及 Netlist 的 DDR5 诉讼。这笔费用不影响非 GAAP 数字，但值得在 10 月中旬的 10-K 里核对。I27

Second, GAAP results include a $500M "patent license charge" with no counterparty named and no mention on either call. In the same period a German court found Micron infringed two YMTC NAND patents (Sep 23, appealable), and Netlist's DDR5 suit is ongoing. The charge does not touch non-GAAP figures, but it is worth checking in the 10-K due in mid-October.

其三，客户押金不进自由现金流。本季收到 SCA 现金押金 $123 亿，期末资产负债表上 $127 亿（非流动“客户合同负债” $128.95 亿）。这些钱计入筹资活动，按协议在合同期后半段逐步退还。所以 $332 亿的 FCF 是“干净”的；但 $735 亿现金里约有 $127 亿是将来要还给客户的。

Third, customer deposits sit outside free cash flow. Micron received $12.3B of SCA cash deposits in the quarter and held $12.7B at quarter end (non-current customer contract liabilities of $12.9B). They run through financing activities and are returned during the back half of each contract. The $33.2B of FCF is therefore clean, but about $12.7B of the $73.5B cash pile is owed back to customers over time.

为什么重要Why it matters

1. 动量是平的，不是在衰减。“营收环比从 +31% 降到 +13%”这类标题会让人以为增长在明显放慢。按周均看，FQ4 与 FQ1 指引都在 +22% 左右，几乎一样快。

2. 卡住增长的是供给。FQ4 DRAM 周均出货位元是负的，同时库存天数从 120 升到 129（老制程停产前的备货 + 激励薪酬资本化）。FQ1 指引的“个位数位元增长”落在 13 周里，周均约 +10% 左右——这部分来自消化库存（管理层预计库存天数“将在未来几个季度下降”）。这和《缺口的算术》v4 的库存耗尽逻辑相互印证：2026 年还能靠库存补位，2027 年这块缓冲基本用完。

3. 一年后的同比会被低估。FY27 Q4 只有 13 周，对着 14 周的基数，同比增速会被机械地压低约 7 个百分点。到时候请记得把这一周加回来。

1. Momentum is flat, not fading. Headlines like "growth slows from +31% to +13%" suggest a sharp deceleration. Per week, FQ4 and the FQ1 guide both run at about +22%.

2. Supply is what binds. DRAM bits shipped per week fell in FQ4 while inventory days rose from 120 to 129 (end-of-life build-ahead plus capitalized incentive pay). The FQ1 guide's "single-digit" bit growth spread over 13 weeks works out to roughly +10% per week — drawn partly from inventory (management expects inventory days "to decline in the coming quarters"). This matches the inventory-exhaustion logic of The Arithmetic of the Gap v4: inventory could fill gaps in 2026; by 2027 that buffer is largely spent.

3. Next year's year-over-year will look worse than it is. FQ4 FY27 has 13 weeks against a 14-week base, which mechanically trims reported YoY growth by about 7 points. Add the week back when that print arrives.

需求弹性出现Demand elasticity has appeared

价格已经开始“配给”需求：MCBU（手机/PC）出货位元连续第二个季度环比下降（BNP 分析师在会上点出，管理层确认，靠价格与组合实现营收增长）；管理层预计 2026 年 PC 与手机出货可能双位数下滑；服务器端客户在降配。存储行业此刻同时处在“配额”（客户要不到货）和“配给”（价格把边际需求挤出）两种状态里。前者支撑久期，后者压低斜率。

Price is now rationing demand. MCBU (phones and PCs) shipped fewer bits sequentially for the second quarter in a row (raised by BNP on the analyst call and confirmed by management; revenue still grew on price and mix). Management expects possible double-digit unit declines in PCs and phones in 2026, and server customers are trimming content. The industry is in "allocation" (customers cannot get supply) and "rationing" (price pushes out marginal demand) at once. The first supports duration; the second flattens the slope.

推论一：利润倒挂——“护城河”产品成了毛利最低的产品Inference 1: the margin inversion — the moat product became the lowest-margin product

HBM 按年度谈价：2026 年的价格在 2025 年就锁定了。此后一般 DRAM 合约价在 1Q26 暴涨 90–95%、2Q26 再涨 58–63%、3Q26 再涨 13–18%（TrendForce），商品产品的价格把 HBM 远远甩在身后。CMBU 的毛利率在 FQ1-26 还是四个单元里最高的（66%），到 FQ4 已经垫底（83% vs 90%）。管理层的原话是 2027 年 HBM 涨价是为了“缩小与常规 DRAM 的毛利差距”——这句话本身就承认了 HBM 目前更不赚钱。

HBM is priced annually: 2026 prices were fixed in 2025. Conventional DRAM contract prices then jumped 90–95% in 1Q26, another 58–63% in 2Q26 and 13–18% in 3Q26 (TrendForce), leaving HBM far behind. CMBU had the best margin of the four units in FQ1-26 (66%); by FQ4 it had the worst (83% vs 90%). Management says the 2027 HBM increases are meant to "narrow the gross margin gap with conventional DRAM" — an admission that HBM currently earns less.

这对 FY27 是一个机械性的利好：2027 年 HBM 价格“在日历年初重置”，TrendForce 估计 2027 年 HBM 混合均价 +121%。FY27 Q2（12 月–2 月）包含了 1、2 月的新价格，这正是管理层敢说“FQ1 是全年毛利底部”的原因。对 HBM 占比最高的 SK 海力士，这个效应更大（第 14 节）。

For FY27 this is a mechanical tailwind. 2027 HBM prices "reset at the beginning of the calendar year", and TrendForce puts the 2027 blended HBM ASP at +121%. FQ2 FY27 (Dec–Feb) carries two months of new pricing — the reason management can call FQ1 the margin floor for the year. The effect is larger still for SK hynix, which has the highest HBM mix (Section 14).

四股力量Four forces

① 重新定价（利好，2027 年起）。TrendForce 把 2027 年 HBM 混合均价预测上调到 +121%，理由是持续的供给约束、HBM4 占比提升和 HBM4E 在 2027 下半年放量；UBS 的口径是 +79%，韩媒是 +50% 以上。即便取最保守的 +50%，叠加位元增长，2027 年 HBM 营收也会显著跳升。对美光而言，这把 CMBU 的毛利从“垫底”拉回到与常规 DRAM 相当，是 FY27 毛利“FQ1 后更高”的主要来源。

(1) Repricing (positive, from 2027). TrendForce raised its 2027 blended HBM ASP forecast to +121%, citing persistent supply constraints, a rising HBM4 mix and HBM4E ramping in 2H27; UBS has +79%, Korean press "over 50%". Even at the low end, combined with bit growth, 2027 HBM revenue jumps sharply. For Micron this pulls CMBU's margin from last place back toward conventional DRAM, and it is the main source of "higher margins beyond FQ1".

② 份额（中性偏负）。Counterpoint 数据显示，2Q26 美光 HBM 营收份额从 21% 降到 18%，三星从 21% 升到 33%（HBM4 销售环比 3 倍以上、HBM4 良率据报道升至约 80%）。美光把目标定为“约等于 DRAM 份额”，说明它不打算用价格换份额。2027 年 HBM 重新变得比常规 DRAM 更赚钱之后，三星把 HBM 占其 DRAM 产量从 27%（2025）提到 40%（2027）的计划，会成为 2028 年 HBM 价格的主要压力。

(2) Share (neutral to negative). Counterpoint shows Micron's HBM revenue share slipping from 21% to 18% in 2Q26 while Samsung rose from 21% to 33% (HBM4 sales up more than 3x sequentially; HBM4 yield reportedly ~80%). Micron's target of "around DRAM share" says it will not buy share with price. Once HBM is again more profitable than conventional DRAM in 2027, Samsung's plan to lift HBM from 27% (2025) to 40% (2027) of its DRAM output becomes the main pressure on 2028 HBM pricing.

③ 降配（短期负、长期中性）。英伟达 Vera Rubin（VR200）仍是每颗 GPU 288GB HBM4（8 颗 12 层 36GB），但 Vera CPU 的 SOCAMM 从 192GB 减到 96GB（单机柜 LPDDR5X 约 55TB→28TB）；Rubin Ultra 原定每封装 1TB HBM4E，现在在评估 8 层 HBM4E、12 层 HBM4 乃至 8 层 HBM4，测试配置 192–288GB；TrendForce 9/29 称 8 层已成为优先选项，且 2027 年 8 层每 Gb 价格比 12 层高 10–20%。原因很直接：内存已占 VR200 物料成本约 29%，高于英伟达约 20% 的舒适区。美银的判断是这只是暂时的量的逆风，因为“低于 500GB 时性能会急剧下降”。

(3) De-spec (negative near term, neutral long term). NVIDIA's Vera Rubin (VR200) still carries 288GB of HBM4 per GPU (8 × 12-high 36GB), but the Vera CPU's SOCAMM went from 192GB to 96GB (rack LPDDR5X ~55TB → ~28TB). Rubin Ultra was planned at 1TB of HBM4E per package; NVIDIA is now evaluating 8-high HBM4E, 12-high HBM4 or even 8-high HBM4, with test configurations of 192–288GB; TrendForce (9/29) says 8-high is emerging as the priority, carrying a 10–20% per-Gb premium over 12-high in 2027. The reason is plain: memory is ~29% of the VR200 bill of materials versus NVIDIA's ~20% comfort level. BofA sees a temporary volume headwind because "performance falls sharply below 500GB".

④ 定制化（价值再分配）。从 HBM4E 开始，基底逻辑芯片转向代工厂（台积电 N12/N3 级；SK 海力士据报在评估英特尔），NVHBM 把内存控制器放进基底芯片，宣称带宽 +30%、HBM 功耗 −15%。这让 HBM 更像“半定制芯片”：存储厂获得更强的客户绑定，但设计价值更多落在英伟达手里，制造价值有一部分流向代工厂。SK 海力士已在 8 月表示混合键合要到 HBM5（约 2029–30 年）才可能采用，TC 热压键合会再用一代。

(4) Customization (value redistribution). From HBM4E the logic base die moves to foundries (TSMC N12/N3-class; SK hynix reportedly weighing Intel), and NVHBM puts the memory controller into the base die, claiming +30% bandwidth and −15% HBM power. That makes HBM more of a semi-custom chip: memory makers get stronger customer lock-in, but more design value accrues to NVIDIA and some manufacturing value to the foundry. SK hynix said in August that hybrid bonding is unlikely before HBM5 (around 2029–30), so thermo-compression bonding lasts another generation.

判断Judgment

2027 年的 HBM 是“价涨、量的增速被压、份额被争”的组合。对美光，价格效应大于份额效应，FY27 HBM 毛利会显著改善。真正需要盯的是 2028 年：届时三星的 HBM 产能、SK 海力士的 M15X/龙仁、美光新加坡封装厂都已投产，而客户已习惯用 8 层与定制基底来控制内存成本——HBM 的定价权可能比常规 DRAM 先松动。这与很多人“HBM 是最安全的部分”的直觉相反。

2027 HBM combines higher prices, capped bit growth and a share fight. For Micron the price effect outweighs the share effect, so FY27 HBM margins improve sharply. The year to watch is 2028: Samsung's added HBM capacity, SK hynix's M15X/Yongin and Micron's Singapore packaging will all be running, and customers will be used to managing memory cost with 8-high stacks and custom base dies. HBM pricing power may loosen before conventional DRAM's does — the opposite of the common intuition that HBM is the safest part of the business.

为什么 AI 需要这么多 SSDWhy AI needs so much SSD

大模型推理在“解码”阶段要反复读取每个历史 token 的 KV 缓存。上下文越长、并发越多、智能体越多轮，KV 缓存越大；放不进 HBM 的部分被卸载到 CPU 内存、再到 SSD。英伟达的 CMX 平台（原称 ICMS，由 BlueField-4 驱动）为每颗 GPU 提供最多 16TB 的闪存层，每个 BlueField-4 挂 150TB NVMe，一个机柜约 9.6PB。若 100 万颗 GPU 都按 16TB 配置，就是约 16EB 的 NAND（本报告的示意算术，渗透率未知）。美光同时在出货面向 KV 缓存的 PCIe Gen5/Gen6 SSD（7600、9650）和大容量 QLC（6600 ION）替代硬盘。A18

During decode, an LLM re-reads the KV cache of every prior token. Longer contexts, more concurrency and more multi-turn agents all grow that cache; what does not fit in HBM spills to CPU memory and then to SSD. NVIDIA's CMX platform (formerly ICMS, powered by BlueField-4) provides up to 16TB of flash per GPU, 150TB of NVMe per BlueField-4 and about 9.6PB per rack. If a million GPUs were provisioned at 16TB each, that would be about 16EB of NAND (illustrative arithmetic; attach rates unknown). Micron is shipping PCIe Gen5/Gen6 SSDs aimed at KV cache (7600, 9650) and high-capacity QLC (6600 ION) for HDD displacement.

NAND 已经分成两个市场：AI 数据中心的 eSSD 在加速，消费与移动端在降温。美光的 NAND 有 2/3 以上在前者，比 Sandisk、Kioxia 更抗跌；它在 2026 年有意让 NAND 位元增速低于行业，也是在守价格。但如果周期要先出裂缝，最可能出在 NAND 现货与消费级合约价上。建议的观察阈值：512Gb TLC 晶圆现货跌破约 $16.9（较 8 月高点 −20%），或 TrendForce 给出 1Q27 NAND 合约价持平/下跌。

NAND has split into two markets: AI data-center eSSD is accelerating while consumer and mobile cool off. Over two-thirds of Micron's NAND is in the former, which makes it sturdier than Sandisk or Kioxia, and Micron deliberately held 2026 NAND bit growth below the industry to protect price. Still, if the cycle cracks first, it most likely cracks in NAND spot and consumer contract prices. 为什么股价交易的是“二阶导”Why the stock trades the second derivative

存储股历来在盈利见顶之前见顶：价格涨幅放缓到接近零、但合约价还没转跌的时候，股价往往已经提前反映。2018 年那一轮，美光股价在 2018 年年中（5–6 月）见顶，盈利在 8 月结束的 FY18Q4 才见顶，DRAM 合约价到 2018 年第四季度才开始下跌。2026 年的节奏相似：一般 DRAM 合约价环比从 1Q26 的 +90–95% 降到 4Q26 预测的 +10–15%；MU 在 6 月 25 日 FQ3 财报当天创下 $1,255 的盘中高点，此后 FY27 一致预期 EPS 从约 $118 升到 $156.5（+33%），股价却比 6/25 收盘低 12%，前瞻市盈率从约 10 倍压缩到 6.8 倍。盈利在上修、估值在下修，这就是“二阶导交易”。

Memory stocks have historically peaked before earnings do: by the time price increases slow toward zero — but before contract prices turn down — the stock has usually priced it. In the 2018 cycle Micron's stock peaked in mid-2018 (May–June), earnings peaked in FQ4 FY18 (ended August), and DRAM contract prices only began falling in 4Q18. 2026 rhymes: conventional DRAM contract increases went from +90–95% in 1Q26 to a forecast +10–15% in 4Q26. MU hit an intraday high of $1,255 on June 25, the day after FQ3; since then FY27 consensus EPS rose from about $118 to $156.5 (+33%) while the stock sits 12% below the June 25 close, compressing the forward P/E from about 10x to 6.8x. Earnings revised up, multiple revised down: that is a second-derivative trade.

12Supply: the 2028 wall

供给侧：为什么 2027 年更紧，以及 2028 年之墙Supply: why 2027 is tighter, and the 2028 wall

本轮紧缺的根源在供给侧的四个物理约束：洁净室、制程增益递减、HBM 的晶圆换算比、库存耗尽。它们共同解释了“资本开支在涨、位元增速却在放缓”。但同一批约束也决定了紧缺的终点：混凝土正在浇筑，2028 年末到 2029 年会集中变成洁净室。 This shortage comes from four physical constraints on supply: cleanroom space, shrinking node gains, HBM's wafer trade ratio and exhausted inventory. Together they explain why bit growth slows while capex rises. The same constraints also set the shortage's end date: concrete poured now turns into cleanrooms in late 2028 and 2029.

  2. 制程增益递减。未来节点每片晶圆带来的位元增益更小，而单位位元成本在上升（LP5→LP6、DDR5→DDR6、HBM 换代）。Shrinking node gains. Future nodes add fewer bits per wafer while cost per bit rises (LP5→LP6, DDR5→DDR6, HBM generations).
  3. HBM 换算比。同等位元下 HBM 占用约 3 倍晶圆；HBM4 更高的堆叠还要再差约三分之一。三星计划把 HBM 占 DRAM 产量从 27%（2025）提到 40%（2027），SK 海力士约 30%（2026）→ 40%（2027，未经证实）；TrendForce 估计 2026 年 HBM + RDIMM 已占 DRAM 位元供给的 51%。HBM trade ratio. Samsung plans HBM at 40% of DRAM output in 2027 (27% in 2025); SK hynix ~30% in 2026 → ~40% in 2027 (unverified); TrendForce puts HBM + RDIMM at 51% of 2026 DRAM bit supply.
  4. 库存耗尽。KB 证券（9/7）估计三星与 SK 海力士的存储库存已降到 10 天以下（正常约 30–45 天），并认为 2027 年需求增速比供给增速高 10 个百分点以上。2026 年还能靠去库存补位，2027 年这块缓冲基本没有了——这正是“2027 比 2026 更紧”在算术上的来源，也是《缺口的算术》v4 的核心。Exhausted inventory. Lay out the timeline. 2027 adds only point capacity: ID1, Tongluo, the M15X ramp, Yongin Y1's first cleanroom, CXMT Shanghai. Late 2028 to 2029 brings Micron's ID2, Hiroshima and Singapore NAND, Samsung P5 phase-1 production, SK hynix M17 and Yongin Y2, plus new YMTC and Nanya capacity — all at once. Management says tools will be added "as demand warrants", which is better discipline than in past cycles; but every past cycle ended when a cluster of new capacity met slowing demand growth. If AI capex growth slows sharply in 2028 from roughly +50% in 2027, the wall becomes the price inflection. Markets usually price 12–18 months ahead, so the real debate window is 2H 2027.

需求端同时存在四股力量：超大规模云厂的资本开支继续高增长；内存涨价正在吞掉这笔钱里越来越大的份额；模型在变得更省内存；手机和 PC 被价格挤出。前两股决定了 2027 年，后两股决定了单位内存含量的斜率。 Four forces act on demand at once: hyperscaler capex keeps growing fast; rising memory prices eat a growing share of it; models get more memory-efficient; phones and PCs get priced out. 净效应：效率让单位 token 的内存下降，但 token 数量的增长快一个数量级；更省内存的模型更便宜，反而被用得更多（杰文斯效应）。美光承认的“内容增速略低于预期”，更像是价格与效率共同作用下的单位含量放缓，而不是总需求下降。真正要警惕的是另一种情形：总 token 增速本身放缓，而效率继续提高——那时单位含量下降会直接变成总需求下降。

Net effect: efficiency cuts memory per token, but token volume grows an order of magnitude faster, and cheaper, frugal models get used more (Jevons). The "modestly lower content growth" Micron conceded looks like price and efficiency slowing content per unit, not falling total demand. The scenario to fear is different: total token growth itself slowing while efficiency keeps improving — then lower content per unit turns directly into lower total demand.

终端：被价格挤出的需求Devices: demand priced out

IDC 预计 2026 年智能手机出货 −16.7%，创历史最大跌幅，均价 +27.6%，$100 以下机型二季度同比约 −60%；入门机的物料成本中内存占比超过 65%。笔记本的 CPU+DRAM+SSD 占物料成本从约 45% 升到 68%（TrendForce），品牌要涨价约 80% 才能保住毛利。三星手机部门二季度出现首次经营亏损（−₩0.7 万亿），小米手机毛利率降到 8.5%，HP 称内存已占 PC 物料成本 35%，任天堂 Switch 2 涨价 $50 仍“无法覆盖全部成本上涨”。汽车有超过 80% 的车型使用 DDR4/LPDDR4，切换需要 12–24 个月。美光管理层的说法是，尽管 PC 与手机出货可能双位数下滑，行业营收今年仍会增长——典型的“量缩价涨”。

IDC expects 2026 smartphone shipments to fall 16.7%, a record, with ASPs up 27.6% and sub-$100 phones down ~60% YoY in 2Q; memory exceeds 65% of the bill of materials on budget phones. In notebooks, CPU+DRAM+SSD rose from ~45% to 68% of BOM (TrendForce); brands would need ~80% price increases to hold margins. Samsung's mobile division posted its first-ever operating loss in 2Q (−₩0.7T), Xiaomi's smartphone gross margin fell to 8.5%, HP says memory is 35% of PC BOM, and Nintendo's $50 Switch 2 increase "will not cover all cost increases". Over 80% of vehicles use DDR4/LPDDR4, and switching takes 12–24 months. Micron says PC and mobile industry revenue still grows this year despite possible double-digit unit declines — classic volume down, price up.

真正的需求风险The real demand risk

本报告认为，2027 年需求端最大的风险不是效率冲击，而是AI 资本开支的融资能力。S&P 估计 2027 年六大资本开支方中只有微软自由现金流为正（Alphabet −$827 亿、亚马逊 −$601 亿、甲骨文 −$416 亿、SpaceX −$1,144 亿）；Meta 二季度自由现金流仅 $7.84 亿；甲骨文 FQ1 自由现金流 −$54 亿并增发 $200 亿股票；美联储 9 月 16 日加息 25bp 至 3.75–4.00%，为 2023 年以来首次。内存税 + 利率上行，是让 AI 资本开支增速放缓最可能的组合；一旦发生，存储作为高 β 的衍生需求会最先被重新定价。

This report's view: the biggest 2027 demand risk is not efficiency but the financing of AI capex. S&P projects only Microsoft with positive free cash flow among the six largest spenders in 2027 (Alphabet −$82.7B, Amazon −$60.1B, Oracle −$41.6B, SpaceX −$114.4B); Meta's Q2 FCF was just $784M; Oracle's FQ1 FCF was −$5.4B alongside a $20B equity raise; and the Fed hiked 25bp to 3.75–4.00% on Sep 16, its first hike since 2023. A memory tax plus rising rates is the likeliest combination to slow AI capex growth — and memory, a high-beta derived demand, would be repriced first.

判断Judgment

本轮的行业纪律确实好于过去：合同化、洁净室先于设备、三家都在说“按需求装机”。但纪律的考验在 2028 年，而不是现在。届时四家（三星、SK 海力士、美光、长鑫）都会有大量新空间，任何一家为了份额先降价，都会改变整个价格曲线。三星是规模上的摇摆者，长鑫是旧制程上的搅局者。

Industry discipline really is better this time: contracts, cleanrooms before tools, all three majors talking about equipping "as demand warrants". The test of discipline comes in 2028, not now. By then all four (Samsung, SK hynix, Micron, CXMT) will have large new space, and any one cutting price for share changes the whole curve. Samsung is the swing player by scale; CXMT is the disruptor in legacy nodes.

十条被低估的线索（按本报告的关注度排序）Ten under-discussed leads (ranked by this report's interest)

  1. “先建房子”：厂房、超纯水与洁净室承包商。美光 FY27 的增量资本开支以建设为主（目标是 2028 年末以后的洁净室），SK 海力士龙仁 Y2/M17 合计约 ₩54.3 万亿、三星 P5 两座同时推进。超纯水系统商野村微科学（Nomura Micro Science）计划订单约 3.5 倍、前瞻市盈率约 12.7 倍；奥加诺（Organo）订单指引 +37%，但 52 周股价仅 +3%；韩国管道承包商 Hanyang ENG 拿下 ₩1,481 亿的 P5 合同，市盈率约 8.3 倍。为什么被忽略：市场盯着设备股，而建设比设备早 12–18 个月。反证：利润率薄（韩国洁净室承包商营业利润率 1–6%）、项目执行与汇率；美国的晶圆厂建设商多为私营公司（柏克德、Hoffman），无法直接参与。

    "Build first": fab construction, ultrapure water and cleanroom contractors. Micron's FY27 capex increase is mostly construction (cleanrooms for late 2028 onward); SK hynix's Yongin Y2/M17 total ~₩54.3T; Samsung is building two P5 fabs at once. Ultrapure-water maker Nomura Micro Science plans ~3.5x orders at ~12.7x forward P/E; Organo guides orders +37% yet is up only ~3% over 52 weeks; Korean piping contractor Hanyang ENG won a ₩148.1B P5 contract at ~8.3x P/E. Why overlooked: the market watches tool makers, but construction leads tools by 12–18 months. What would disprove it: thin margins (1–6% operating margins at Korean cleanroom firms), execution and FX; US fab builders are mostly private (Bechtel, Hoffman), so not investable.

  2. 2027 年 HBM 重新定价对 SK 海力士的弹性。HBM 份额 50%、2Q26 DRAM ASP 只涨 30%（被年度定价压住）。2027 年 1 月重置后（TrendForce 混合均价 +121%），它是机械受益最大的一家，而韩股前瞻市盈率仅约 4.3 倍。反证：2028 年三星 HBM 扩产压价；客户转向 8 层与定制基底；韩国市场的政策与资金折价。

    SK hynix's leverage to the 2027 HBM repricing. 50% HBM share and only +30% DRAM ASP in 2Q26 (held back by annual pricing). After the January 2027 reset (TrendForce: blended ASP +121%) it benefits most mechanically, at a forward P/E of about 4.3x. What would disprove it: Samsung's HBM expansion pressuring 2028 prices; customers shifting to 8-high and custom base dies; Korea's policy and flow discount.

  3. 客户在为旧制程“预付”。Sandisk、Kioxia、Solidigm、思科合计认购南亚科 10.19% 股权并签 2028 年后的供货；华邦在谈 3–5 年预付长约；NOR 与 SLC NAND 上半年涨 100–150%、下半年预计再涨 60–75%。两家前瞻市盈率约 4–5 倍。反证：长鑫在 DDR4/LPDDR4 上的扩张；两家自己的新产能 2028 年到位；周期股的低市盈率本身就是“峰值”的定价。

    Customers are prepaying for legacy capacity. Sandisk, Kioxia, Solidigm and Cisco together bought 10.19% of Nanya with supply beyond 2028; Winbond is negotiating 3–5-year prepaid deals; NOR and SLC NAND rose 100–150% in 1H26 with another 60–75% expected in 2H. Both trade at ~4–5x forward. What would disprove it: CXMT's DDR4/LPDDR4 expansion; their own new capacity in 2028; low cyclical multiples are themselves a peak price.

  4. “QLC 杀死硬盘”的逻辑被反转了。NAND 紧缺让近线硬盘同样缺货：希捷产能分配到 2028 年、资本开支仅占营收 4–6%；西数在谈 2029–2031 年长约。但这已不是“被忽略”：希捷 52 周 +302%。真正被忽略的是时间点——2028 年下半年 NAND 新厂（美光新加坡、SK 海力士 M17）投产后，替代逻辑会重新启动。

    "QLC kills HDD" got inverted. The NAND shortage made nearline drives short too: Seagate is allocated into 2028 with capex at 4–6% of revenue; WDC is negotiating 2029–2031 LTAs. No longer overlooked in price, though: Seagate is up 302% in 52 weeks. What is overlooked is timing — substitution restarts once new NAND fabs (Micron Singapore, SK hynix M17) open in 2H28.

  5. 混合键合推迟一代，TC 键合机多吃一代。SK 海力士 8 月表示 HBM4E 不用混合键合，最早到 HBM5（约 2029–30）；JEDEC 把高度上限放宽到 775µm 是直接原因。ASMPT 订单 +97.6%、有 SK 海力士 HBM4 的 TC 份额；Hanmi 已被充分定价（约 45 倍）且份额在流失；BESI 是存储端的“近期输家”（逻辑端仍增长）。HBF（高带宽闪存）还会给 TC 键合带来 NAND 堆叠的新需求。

    Hybrid bonding slipped a generation; TC bonders get one more. SK hynix said in August that HBM4E will not use hybrid bonding, with HBM5 (~2029–30) the earliest; JEDEC relaxing the height limit to 775µm is the direct cause. ASMPT's bookings rose 97.6% and it holds an SK hynix HBM4 TC position; Hanmi is fully priced (~45x) and losing share; BESI is the near-term memory loser (logic still grows). High Bandwidth Flash adds new NAND-stacking demand for TC bonders.

  6. 探针卡与测试的相对价值。HBM 每颗要测更多颗粒，探针卡与测试机是随产量放大的“耗材”。Micronics Japan（MJC）DRAM 纯度更高，前瞻市盈率约 21 倍，而 FormFactor 约 40 倍；Techwing 拿到美光封装厂订单（11 月交付），约 16 倍；Technoprobe 的 HBM 占比只有约 2%，被误贴了 HBM 标签。反证：测试需求在新厂出片后才放量，时间点在 2027–28。

    Relative value in probe cards and test. HBM multiplies the dies tested per stack, so probe cards and testers scale like consumables. Micronics Japan (MJC) is a purer DRAM play at ~21x forward vs FormFactor at ~40x; Techwing won Micron packaging orders (delivery in November) at ~16x; Technoprobe's HBM exposure is only ~2%, a mislabel. What would disprove it: test demand scales only once new fabs produce wafers, in 2027–28.

  7. 上游的滞后重新定价。2023–24 年签的硅片长约陆续到期，报价自 7 月 1 日起上调；HBM 每位元消耗约 3 倍晶圆。Resonac 的 NCF/导热材料/塑封料（约 40–50% 份额）几乎贡献了全部核心利润，却被石化业务压着估值（约 21 倍）。反证：中国 300mm 硅片产能份额从 28% 升到 32%；石化拖累。

    Upstream repricing with a lag. Silicon-wafer LTAs signed in 2023–24 are expiring and quotes rose from July 1; HBM consumes ~3x the wafer per bit. Resonac's NCF, thermal and mold materials (~40–50% share) generate nearly all its core profit, but petrochemicals depress the multiple (~21x). What would disprove it: China's share of 300mm wafer capacity rising from 28% to 32%; the petrochemical drag.

  8. 内存接口芯片是这轮的掉队者。Rambus 52 周 −0.8%（同期 Sandisk +1,433%）：它按模组收费，而模组数量被 DRAM 供给卡住；澜起的经常性利润只增 15–33%。2027 年 MRDIMM 放量、2028 年新厂带来更多模组，是可能的追赶点。反证：7 月韩国检方搜查 Rambus、澜起、瑞萨（涉嫌对三星/SK 海力士串通定价，三家合计份额 >93%），赔偿或降价风险。

    Memory-interface chips missed this cycle. Rambus is −0.8% over 52 weeks (Sandisk +1,433%): it is paid per module, and module counts are capped by DRAM supply; Montage's recurring profit grew only 15–33%. MRDIMM volumes in 2027 and more modules from new fabs in 2028 are possible catch-up points. What would disprove it: Korean prosecutors raided Rambus, Montage and Renesas in July over alleged price collusion (>93% combined share) — damages or price concessions.

  9. 中国的并行存储资本开支。长鑫 IPO 募资 ¥579 亿、长江存储拟募 ¥330 亿；长江三期国产设备占比 >50%；汇丰预计北方华创存储订单 +50% 以上。对非中国设备商意味着份额流失（Lam 的中国收入占比从 34% 降到 26%）。反证：出口管制、A 股估值（约 43 倍 2028E）。

    China's parallel memory capex. CXMT raised ¥57.9B and YMTC seeks ¥33B; YMTC's Fab 3 uses >50% domestic tools; HSBC sees NAURA's memory orders up 50%+. For non-Chinese toolmakers this means share loss (Lam's China revenue share fell from 34% to 26%). What would disprove it: export controls; A-share valuations (~43x 2028E).

  10. 短缺让“内存回收”和池化变得有价值。Marvell 把“DDR4 回收”列为 CXL 的首要用例；英伟达 CMX 把 KV 缓存放到以太网挂载的闪存层。反证：2028 年后若内存转为过剩，回收的经济性消失。

    The shortage makes memory recycling and pooling valuable. Marvell calls "DDR4 recycling" the leading CXL use case; NVIDIA's CMX moves KV cache onto an Ethernet-attached flash tier. What would disprove it: if memory turns to surplus after 2028, recycling economics vanish.

一个反直觉的观察A counter-intuitive observation

市场为“铲子”付 30–55 倍，为“金子”只付 4–8 倍。这只有在供应链的盈利比存储厂更持久时才合理。确实，建设与装机会让设备与材料商的收入至少增长到 2028 年（TEL 预计 2027 年 WFE 超过 $1,900 亿），而存储厂的盈利在 2028–29 年面临拐点。但存储资本开支历来是半导体设备里最周期性的部分：2019 和 2023 年，存储设备订单都在存储价格转跌后的两三个季度内大幅下降。4–8 倍的存储厂已经在给下行定价；30–55 倍的供应链还没有。如果 2028 之墙兑现，风险更可能藏在高倍数的那一端。

The market pays 30–55x for the shovels and 4–8x for the gold. That only makes sense if supply-chain earnings outlast memory makers'. They may, for a while: construction and tool move-ins should grow equipment and materials revenue at least into 2028 (TEL sees 2027 WFE above $190B), while memory makers face an inflection in 2028–29. But memory capex is historically the most cyclical slice of semiconductor equipment: in 2019 and 2023, memory tool orders fell sharply within two or three quarters of memory prices turning down. Memory makers at 4–8x already price a downturn; the supply chain at 30–55x does not. If the 2028 wall arrives, the risk more likely sits at the high-multiple end.

八个含义Eight implications

  1. 内存成了 AI 扩张的“限速器”。英伟达预计 FY28 营收增长约 70%，并直言这是“受供给约束的展望，不受约束会高得多”；它的 $2,790 亿供应承诺“主要与内存采购有关”。当限速器是内存，AI 扩张会被摊得更平、更长——过度建设的风险反而下降。这对 AI 周期是稳定器，对存储厂是久期。

    Memory has become the speed limiter on AI build-out. NVIDIA expects ~70% FY28 revenue growth and says plainly it is "a supply-constrained outlook; the unconstrained would be a lot higher"; its $279B of supply commitments are "primarily related to the procurement of memory". With memory as the limiter, the build-out is spread flatter and longer, and overbuild risk falls. That stabilizes the AI cycle and lengthens memory's.

  2. 利润正在从算力流向内存。GPU 占 AI 服务器成本从 80% 以上降到约一半，内存约占高端机柜 25–29%；美光毛利率 87%、SK 海力士营业利润率 76%，都高于英伟达的毛利率（75%，并将降到 71–72%）。这是 AI 时代第一次出现存储厂的利润率高于算力龙头。

    Profit is moving from compute to memory. GPUs fell from over 80% of AI server cost to about half; memory is ~25–29% of a high-end rack; Micron's 87% gross margin and SK hynix's 76% operating margin both exceed NVIDIA's gross margin (75%, heading to 71–72%). For the first time in the AI era, memory makers out-earn the compute leader on margin.

  3. “每 token 字节数”成为和 FLOPs 同等重要的指标。稀疏 MoE（DeepSeek 552B 参数只激活 8–16B）、KV 压缩（TurboQuant 约 6 倍；DeepSeek V4.1 约 890 字节/token）、FP4、预填充/解码分离（Rubin CPX 用 GDDR7）、SRAM 解码、上下文存储层（CMX）、HBF——模型与系统架构正在被内存的价格塑形。“内存墙”已经从带宽问题变成了经济问题。

    "Bytes per token" becomes as important as FLOPs. Sparse MoE (DeepSeek activates 8–16B of 552B), KV compression (TurboQuant ~6x; DeepSeek V4.1 at ~890 bytes/token), FP4, prefill/decode disaggregation (GDDR7 on Rubin CPX), SRAM decode, context-storage tiers (CMX), HBF — model and system design are being shaped by the price of memory. The memory wall has gone from a bandwidth problem to an economic one.

  4. 稀缺让 AI 算力更集中。能预付、能签五年长约的是资产负债表最强的买家（微软、谷歌、亚马逊、Meta、英伟达）；小型实验室、新云和主权 AI 只能付现货价或排队。CoreWeave 7 月甚至研究过对冲“内存价格下跌”。内存短缺在客观上加固了头部玩家的领先。

    Scarcity concentrates AI compute. The buyers that can prepay and sign five-year deals have the strongest balance sheets (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta, NVIDIA); smaller labs, neoclouds and sovereign AI pay spot or wait. CoreWeave even explored hedging against falling memory prices in July. The shortage entrenches the leaders.

  5. 端侧 AI 被拖慢，物理 AI 是下一条腿。手机配置在倒退（12GB/256GB → 8GB/128GB 的同价替换），低端机被挤出；高端（苹果）反而在涨份额。L4 自动驾驶内存 >200GB、存储数 TB，人形机器人相当——但它们成为规模需求的时间（本世纪末）恰好与 2028–29 年的供给潮重叠。

    On-device AI slows; physical AI is the next leg. Phone specs are going backwards (12GB/256GB swapped for 8GB/128GB at the same price) and the low end is priced out, while premium (Apple) gains share. L4 vehicles need >200GB of DRAM and terabytes of storage, humanoids similar — but they become volume demand around the end of the decade, overlapping the 2028–29 supply wave.

  6. 存储周期与信用周期绑在了一起。内存涨价提高了每 GW 的资本需求（英伟达系统涨价至少带来每 GW 约 $50 亿的额外成本，分析师估计），而云厂自由现金流在转负、美联储在加息。AI 资本开支越来越依赖债务、租赁与预付——下一次存储下行，更可能由融资条件而不是技术触发。

    The memory cycle is now tied to the credit cycle. Memory inflation raises the capital needed per gigawatt (NVIDIA's system price increases add at least ~$5B per GW, by analyst estimates), while hyperscaler free cash flow turns negative and the Fed hikes. AI capex leans more on debt, leases and prepayments — the next memory downturn is more likely triggered by financing conditions than by technology.

  7. 短缺在为自己的解药付钱。创纪录的资本开支（部分由客户押金出资）+ 算法效率，会在 2029–30 年前后让瓶颈松动。但合同结构与半定制化可能让存储在 AI 价值中的份额，长期高于 2010 年代的水平。

    The shortage is paying for its own cure. Record capex (partly funded by customer deposits) plus algorithmic efficiency should loosen the bottleneck around 2029–30. Contract structures and semi-customization may still keep memory's share of AI value structurally above its 2010s level.

  8. 投资重心的迁移。AI 交易的重心已经从 GPU 移到存储、存储系统与电力。下一次迁移可能指向“谁能缓解内存瓶颈”：先进封装与基底芯片、HBF 与 CXL 池化、内存效率高的模型与推理系统。

    Where the AI trade moves next. Its center of gravity has shifted from GPUs to memory, storage and power. The next shift may favor whoever relieves the memory bottleneck: advanced packaging and base dies, HBF and CXL pooling, and memory-efficient models and inference systems.

一句话In one line

如果说 2023–24 年 AI 的瓶颈是 GPU，2025–26 年是 CoWoS 与电力，那么 2026–28 年的瓶颈是内存；而内存瓶颈最终会被资本（新厂）和算法（更省内存的模型）同时解决——问题只是哪一个先到。

If AI's bottleneck in 2023–24 was GPUs and in 2025–26 CoWoS and power, in 2026–28 it is memory — and the memory bottleneck will eventually be solved by capital (new fabs) and algorithms (more frugal models) at once. The only question is which arrives first.
