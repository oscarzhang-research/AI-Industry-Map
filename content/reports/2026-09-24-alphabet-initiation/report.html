Alphabet：加速中的印钞机，押上史无前例的资本开支Alphabet: an accelerating cash machine placing a capex bet of historic size

问题不再是"谷歌是不是好生意"，而是"每年约2,000亿美元以上的投入能赚回多少"。The question is no longer whether Google is a great business, but what roughly $200 billion a year of investment will earn.

经营层面，Alphabet正处在近五年最强的状态：2026年Q2收入增长24%，搜索收入在"AI聊天会取代搜索"的担忧中反而加速到17%–19%，云业务增长82%、经营利润率35.6%、未确认合同积压达5,140亿美元。资本层面，它进入了与自身历史完全不同的阶段：2026年资本开支指引1,950–2,050亿美元（约占收入四成），Q2自由现金流转负，回购暂停，6月完成847.5亿美元的股权融资，长期债务半年内翻倍。与此同时，SpaceX上市与Anthropic估值上调让上半年GAAP每股收益多出约8.6美元的账面收益。Operationally, Alphabet is in its strongest shape in five years: Q2 2026 revenue grew 24%, Search accelerated to 17–19% growth despite fears that AI chat would replace it, and Cloud grew 82% with a 35.6% operating margin and a $514 billion contracted backlog. Financially, it has entered a regime unlike its own history: 2026 capex guidance of $195–205 billion (about 40% of revenue), negative free cash flow in Q2, buybacks paused, an $84.75 billion equity raise in June, and long-term debt doubled in six months. Meanwhile, the SpaceX IPO and Anthropic's markup added roughly $8.6 of paper gains to first-half GAAP EPS.

多空分歧（差异化认知）Where bulls and bears disagree

看多的理由The bull case

  • 搜索没有被AI吃掉，反而被AI放大：查询量创新高，AI Mode月活超10亿，搜索收入增速从Q1'25的约10%升到Q1'26的19%。AI amplified Search instead of eating it: record queries, AI Mode above 1B monthly users, Search growth up from ~10% in Q1'25 to 19% in Q1'26.
  • 云业务从"追赶者"变成"算力稀缺时代的供给方"：积压5,140亿美元，利润率35%+，TPU开始外销，打开英伟达式的第二曲线。Cloud turned from laggard into a supplier in a compute-scarce era: $514B backlog, 35%+ margin, and TPUs sold externally open an Nvidia-like second curve.
  • 全栈成本优势：自研TPU让单位token成本更低，苹果选择Gemini驱动新Siri。Full-stack cost advantage: in-house TPUs lower the cost per token; Apple chose Gemini to power the new Siri.
  • 约2,180亿美元的SpaceX股权与以Anthropic为主的私募股权，提供额外价值与未来流动性。About $218B of SpaceX shares and private stakes (mostly Anthropic) add value and future liquidity.

看空的理由The bear case

  • 资本开支/收入升至约四成，自由现金流转负；2027年资本开支还将"显著增加"，折旧浪潮尚未到来。Capex is ~40% of revenue and FCF turned negative; 2027 capex will "increase significantly" and the depreciation wave has not arrived yet.
  • 需求集中且带有循环：据报道Alphabet承诺向Anthropic投资最高400亿美元，Anthropic承诺5年内向谷歌云采购约2,000亿美元，而Alphabet同时按新估值确认浮盈。Demand is concentrated and partly circular: Alphabet reportedly committed up to $40B to Anthropic, Anthropic reportedly committed ~$200B over five years to Google Cloud, and Alphabet books gains at the new valuation.
  • 从回购到发股：资本回报叙事改变；6.25%强制可转换优先股还附带以其股息足额支付为前提的分红/回购限制条款。From buybacks to issuance: the capital-return story changed, and the 6.25% mandatory convertible preferred carries dividend-blocker terms that tie common dividends and buybacks to paying the preferred in full.
  • 5%附近的十年期国债收益率下，1.3%的自由现金流收益率要求很长时间的高增长兑现。With the 10-year near 5%, a 1.3% FCF yield requires a long runway of high growth to pay off.
一句话结论Bottom line

这是一家护城河很宽、执行力很强、正在把自己改造成"AI基础设施公司"的企业。以当前约$335的价格，基准情形下它大致被合理定价、略偏贵；决定未来回报的不是广告，而是资本开支的回报率与需求的集中度。初研建议：把它当作高质量核心资产持续跟踪，用本报告第九节的可证伪指标决定加减，而不是用市盈率。This is a wide-moat, well-executed company rebuilding itself into an AI infrastructure provider. Around $335, it looks roughly fairly valued to slightly rich in our base case; future returns will be decided less by advertising than by the return on capex and the concentration of the demand behind it. Initial stance: track it as a high-quality core asset and let the falsifiable indicators in Section 9, not the P/E, drive position changes.

  • 发生了什么：与一年前相比，云增速从32%升到82%；季度资本开支从$224亿升到$449亿；自由现金流从正转负；股东回报从回购变为发股；GAAP利润被投资浮盈显著抬高。What happened: versus a year ago, Cloud growth went from 32% to 82%; quarterly capex from $22.4B to $44.9B; free cash flow from positive to negative; capital return from buybacks to issuance; GAAP profit inflated by investment gains.

搜索：AI没有吃掉它，至少目前还没有Search: AI has not eaten it, at least not yet

搜索收入可以拆成：查询量 × 广告覆盖率 × 点击率 × 单次点击价格，再减去分给苹果、三星、浏览器等渠道的流量获取成本（TAC）。AI改变的是每一项：AI Overviews和AI Mode让查询更长、更复杂，可能减少"蓝色链接"上的自然点击，但也创造了新的广告位和更高商业意图的会话。Search revenue decomposes into queries × ad coverage × click-through × cost per click, minus the traffic acquisition cost (TAC) paid to Apple, Samsung, browsers and others. AI changes every term: AI Overviews and AI Mode make queries longer and more complex, which can reduce organic clicks on blue links but also creates new ad inventory and more commercially intent sessions.

04护城河：深度与持久度Moat: depth and durability

按手册的定义，护城河是长期获得高于资本成本回报的能力，用ROIC与WACC之差的大小和持续时间来衡量，而不是毛利率。我们用经营资本口径估算了Alphabet的ROIC：投入资本 = 应收 + 其他流动资产（含存货）+ 固定资产 + 经营租赁资产 + 商誉与无形资产 − 非有息流动负债 − 经营租赁负债；剔除全部现金、有价证券和非流通股权投资。NOPAT = 经营利润 ×（1 − 约17%的有效税率）。Following the handbook, a moat is the ability to earn returns above the cost of capital for a long time, measured by the size and duration of the ROIC–WACC spread, not by gross margin. We estimate Alphabet's ROIC on operating capital: invested capital = receivables + other current assets (incl. inventory) + PP&E + operating lease assets + goodwill and intangibles − non-interest-bearing current liabilities − operating lease liabilities, excluding all cash, marketable and non-marketable investments. NOPAT = operating income × (1 − ~17% effective tax rate).

C. 方法说明C. Methodology notes

  • 核心EPS：GAAP稀释EPS减去公司在业绩公告脚注中披露的"股权证券收益对每股收益的影响"。FY2025按246亿美元证券收益与约21%税率估算。Core EPS: GAAP diluted EPS minus the per-share effect of equity-security gains disclosed in the release footnotes. FY2025 is estimated from $24.6B of securities gains at ~21% tax.
  • ROIC：见第4节定义；NOPAT税率2024年16.4%、2025年16.8%、TTM取17%；"平均"口径取期初与期末投入资本的平均值。增量ROIC = ΔNOPAT / Δ投入资本（2024年12月→2026年6月）。ROIC: defined in Section 4; NOPAT tax rates of 16.4% (2024), 16.8% (2025) and 17% (TTM); "average" uses the mean of opening and closing invested capital. Incremental ROIC = ΔNOPAT / Δinvested capital (Dec 2024 → Jun 2026).
  • 企业价值：市值 + 长期债务 + 强制可转换优先股（清算优先权约193亿美元）− 现金与有价证券（其中含SpaceX持股）− 私募股权投资。经营租赁未计入。Enterprise value: market cap + long-term debt + mandatory convertible preferred (~$19.3B liquidation preference) − cash and marketable securities (which include the SpaceX stake) − private equity stakes. Operating leases are excluded.
  • DCF：估值日2026年6月30日；2026年仅计下半年（50%）现金流，按0.25年折现；2027–2035年按年中惯例折现；终值在9.5年处折现。经营性净现金 = 现金与有价证券 − SpaceX持股 − 长期债务 ≈ 503亿美元。模型不含营运资本变化，采用"净资本开支"一个变量同时代表资本开支与折旧的差额。DCF: valuation date 30 June 2026; 2026 counts only the second half (50%) of cash flow, discounted 0.25 years; 2027–2035 use mid-year discounting; terminal value is discounted 9.5 years. Operating net cash = cash and marketable securities − SpaceX stake − long-term debt ≈ $50.3B. The model omits working-capital changes and uses one variable, net capex, for the gap between capex and depreciation.
  • 数据可信度分级：公司文件（10-K/10-Q/8-K/代理声明）为一级；法院文书与监管机构公告为一级；主流媒体对公司或文件的转述为二级；第三方估算（搜索份额、AI助手份额、一致预期）为三级，文中已标注。Source tiers: company filings (10-K/10-Q/8-K/proxy) are tier one, as are court and regulator documents; mainstream media relaying company statements or filings are tier two; third-party estimates (search share, AI-assistant share, consensus) are tier three and flagged in the text.
