核心判断（研究观点，非投资建议）Bottom line (research view, not investment advice)

SCA 是“照付不议的多年量约 + 价格区间 + 可退还的履约保证金”。押金买的是“供货资格与价格区间”，不是货本身：每一批货仍按当季在区间内谈定的价格开票确认收入；押金记为非流动负债（$12.9B）、走筹资现金流（$12.7B），既不进收入、也不进美光口径的自由现金流，只通过利息收入（目前约 $0.5B/年）影响利润，并在合约后半段客户完成最低采购量后退还。

上一份研报的“推论二”方向对、程度被高估。按三种独立方法交叉估算，HBM 只占 FQ3 CMBU 的约 20%、FQ4 的约 23%（区间 16–30%），而不是 40–60%。据此，CMBU 中的超大客户常规 DRAM 在 FQ4 环比约 +14%（周均约 +6%），而美光其余 DRAM 为 +34%：上限（ceiling）开始生效的迹象存在，但“几乎持平”的结论应撤回。

保底价约为 FQ4 FY26 综合售价的 0.45 倍（0.35–0.55）；仅以押金作为违约成本时，市场价需较 FQ4 下跌约 64%–71% 客户才有经济动机违约，大致相当于价格回到 FY25 Q4 水平。在那个临界点上，按我们的成本路径，美光毛利率仍约 65%，FY28–30 年营收约 $100–140B、净利约 $50–70B。保底价能防住正常下行，防不住一次完整的价格“回程”。

常态化（FY30–31 均值）盈利的分布很宽：基准（沿用 10/4 DCF 的基准价格路径，即 2028–29 年新产能集中释放）约营收 $205B、毛利率 76%、净利 $123B；软着陆约 $253B / 80% / $163B；压力情景（价格完整回程、保底价被重谈）约 $110B / 64% / $52B；短缺延续约 $470B / 87% / $338B。决定性变量是“价格回落到 FQ4 水平的多少”，SCA 保底价只在压力情景里起作用。

An SCA is a multi-year take-or-pay volume commitment, a price band, and a refundable performance bond. The deposit buys the right to supply and the band, not the goods: every shipment is still invoiced at that quarter's in-band price and booked as revenue. The deposit sits as a noncurrent liability ($12.9B) and arrives as a financing inflow ($12.7B). It never touches revenue or Micron's definition of free cash flow, affects profit only through interest income (about $0.5B a year today), and is returned in the back half of each term once the customer has met its minimum purchases.

Our previous "Inference 2" had the right direction but overstated the size. Three independent methods put HBM at about 20% of CMBU in FQ3 and about 23% in FQ4 (range 16–30%), not 40–60%. On that basis hyperscaler conventional DRAM inside CMBU grew about +14% q/q in FQ4 (about +6% per week) against +34% for the rest of Micron's DRAM. There are signs that ceilings began to bind, but the "roughly flat" conclusion should be withdrawn.

The floor sits at roughly 0.45x the FQ4 FY26 blended price (0.35–0.55). If forfeiting the deposit were the only cost of walking away, customers would gain from default only after market prices fell about 64–71% below FQ4 levels, roughly a round trip to FY25 Q4 prices. At that threshold, on our cost path, Micron would still earn a gross margin near 65%, with FY28–30 revenue of about $100–140B and net income of about $50–70B. The floors protect against an ordinary downturn, not against a full round trip.

Normalized (FY30–31 average) earnings span a wide range: the base case (our Oct 4 DCF's base price path, with new capacity landing in 2028–29) gives about $205B of revenue, a 76% gross margin and $123B of net income; a soft landing about $253B / 80% / $163B; stress (a full price round trip with floors renegotiated) about $110B / 64% / $52B; a persistent shortage about $470B / 87% / $338B. The decisive variable is how far prices fall relative to FQ4. SCA floors matter only in the stress case.

02 / SCA ANATOMY

SCA 到底是什么：一份“照付不议 + 价格区间 + 保证金”的五年合约What an SCA actually is: a five-year take-or-pay contract with a price band and a bond

SCA（Strategic Customer Agreement，战略客户协议）是美光 2026 年起推出的多年期供货协议。它和过去一年一签的长约（LTA）有三点本质区别：期限拉长到约 5 年、数量变成“照付不议”（take-or-pay，不提货也要付钱）、并且客户要交一笔可退还的财务承诺（主要是现金押金）。

SCAs (Strategic Customer Agreements) are the multi-year supply contracts Micron introduced in 2026. They differ from the old one-year LTAs in three ways: terms of about five years, take-or-pay volumes (the customer pays even if it does not take the product), and a refundable financial commitment, mostly cash deposits.

03 / THE DEPOSIT

押金为什么要退：它买的是“资格与区间”，不是货Why the deposit comes back: it buys a place in line and a price band, not the goods

你的直觉是对的：交了押金才有资格拿货，拿多少货就付多少钱。缺的一环是：押金的作用是担保“剩下还没履行的那部分义务”。义务越履行越少，需要的担保也越少，所以在合约后半段退还。

Your intuition is right: the deposit is what qualifies a customer for supply, and the customer pays for whatever it takes. The missing piece is that the deposit secures the part of the obligation not yet performed. As the obligation is performed, less security is needed, so the deposit is returned in the back half.

退还节奏让担保比例“越往后越高”Back-loaded refunds make the cover rise over the term

用一个最简单的例子：5 年最低合同价值 100（每年 20），押金 21，在第 3、4、5 年末各退 1/3。每年初，押金占“剩余最低价值”的比例依次为 21% → 26% → 35% → 35% → 35%。也就是说，退还机制设计成担保比例始终不低于签约时的水平（推断）。但请注意：今天美光实际拿到手的现金只有 $12.7B，约为 RPO 的 8.5%，其余约 $19B 尚待到账或以信用证形式存在。

Take the simplest case: five years of minimum value of 100 (20 a year), a deposit of 21, and refunds of one-third at the end of years 3, 4 and 5. At the start of each year the deposit covers 21% → 26% → 35% → 35% → 35% of the remaining minimum value. The refund schedule therefore keeps cover at or above the signing ratio throughout (inference). Note, though, that Micron has actually received only $12.7B in cash, about 8.5% of RPO; roughly $19B is still to arrive or sits as letters of credit.

06 / FLOOR DECODER

保底价是怎么推出来的：RPO 本质上是“承诺比特 × 保底价”How the floor is inferred: RPO is committed bits times the floor price

美光没有公布保底价，但公布了足够多的边界条件。关键是想清楚 RPO 的定义：最低承诺量 × 最低价格。所以只要估出“承诺了多少比特”，就能把保底价反推出来；价格路径的假设反而不重要。

Micron has not disclosed its floors, but it has disclosed enough boundary conditions. The key is RPO's definition: minimum committed volume × minimum price. Estimate how many bits are committed and the floor follows; assumptions about the future price path matter much less.

方法一（RPO 反推）：保底价 ÷ FQ4 价格 = RPO ÷ [年化量（按 FQ4 价格计） × 2026/9–2030/12 累计量系数 × SCA 比特占比 × 有定价框架占比 × 最低量/预期量] Method 1 (RPO decode): floor ÷ FQ4 price = RPO ÷ [annual volume (at FQ4 prices) × cumulative-volume factor for Sep-2026 to Dec-2030 × SCA bit share × priced share × minimum/expected volume]
方法二（毛利锚）：保底价 ÷ FQ4 价格 = 单位成本占 FQ4 价格的比例 ÷ (1 − 保底毛利率)；管理层称保底毛利率“显著高于以往任何周期峰值”（约 61%，FY18 Q4）。 Method 2 (margin anchor): floor ÷ FQ4 price = unit cost as a share of the FQ4 price ÷ (1 − floor gross margin). Management says floor margins are "meaningfully above any prior cycle peak" (about 61%, FY18 Q4).[3,37]

"Normalized" here means what Micron can earn each year once prices come down from the 2026–27 scarcity peak. The model starts from the actual FQ4 FY26 structure and splits revenue into conventional DRAM, NAND, HBM and other. Each is bits × realized price, with SCA floors and ceilings applied directly to the realized price. Costs split into cash cost per bit plus depreciation from capex. SCA 其实是一份“领口期权”：上行时付保费，下行时才拿赔付An SCA is really a collar: Micron pays a premium on the way up and collects only in a deep downturn

把模型里 SCA 覆盖设为 0，与有 SCA 的结果相比，就能看出价格框架本身的得失。上限价（约 1.0 倍 FQ4 价格）在 2027–28 年高于市价时让美光少收钱；保底价（0.45 倍）只有在价格跌破约 0.45 倍时才起作用。结论很直观：除非真的出现深度下行且保底价被严格执行，SCA 的价格条款在 FY27–31 合计对营收是负贡献。美光换来的是比特销路的确定性、$32B 的无息（推断）资金和客户锁定，而不是更高的利润。

Setting SCA coverage to zero in the model and comparing shows what the pricing terms are worth on their own. The ceiling (about 1.0x the FQ4 price) costs Micron revenue in 2027–28 while the market sits above it; the floor (0.45x) only pays out once prices fall below about 0.45x. The result is intuitive: unless a deep downturn arrives and the floors are strictly enforced, SCA pricing terms subtract from FY27–31 revenue in total. What Micron gets in exchange is certainty of selling its bits, $32B of (probably) interest-free money and customer lock-in, not higher profit.

08 / DEFAULT THRESHOLD

违约临界点：价格跌多少，客户才划算地“弃约”The default threshold: how far prices must fall before walking away pays

客户的账很简单：继续履约，要按保底价买最低量，比市价多付 (保底价 − 市价) × 剩余最低量；违约，最直接的损失是被扣的押金。当多付的钱超过押金（加上其他违约成本）时，违约在经济上划算。

The customer's arithmetic is simple. Honoring the contract means buying the minimum volume at the floor, overpaying by (floor − market) × remaining minimum volume. Defaulting costs, most directly, the forfeited deposit. Once the overpayment exceeds the deposit plus any other cost of default, walking away pays.

临界市价 m* = 保底价 × (1 − 押金覆盖率 − 其他违约成本) ÷ 客户对货物的利用率；押金覆盖率 = 剩余押金 ÷ 剩余最低合同价值 Critical market price m* = floor × (1 − deposit cover − other default costs) ÷ customer's use value of the goods; deposit cover = remaining deposit ÷ remaining minimum contract value

按默认参数（保底价 0.45 倍、押金为 RPO 的 21%、第 3–5 年各退 1/3），押金覆盖率在第 1 年是 21%，第 3 年起升到 35%，对应临界市价约为 FQ4 价格的 0.36 倍（跌 64%）至 0.29 倍（跌 71%）。作为参照，FY25 Q4 的 DRAM 价格约为 FQ4 FY26 的 0.27 倍；UBS 估算价格需在合约期内下跌约 70–80% 毁约才划算。[39] 但如果下行发生在押金尚未收齐之前（今天到账现金只有 RPO 的 8.5%），临界点会浅得多：约跌 59%。

On default settings (floor 0.45x, deposits 21% of RPO, one-third refunded in each of years 3–5), deposit cover is 21% in year 1 and 35% from year 3, putting the critical market price at about 0.36x the FQ4 price (a 64% fall) to 0.29x (71%). For reference, FY25 Q4 DRAM prices were about 0.27x FQ4 FY26, and UBS estimates prices would need to fall about 70–80% over a contract's life before breaking it pays.[39] If the downturn arrives before the deposits are fully collected (cash received today is only 8.5% of RPO), the threshold is much shallower: a fall of about 59%.

怎么读这个临界点How to read the threshold
  • 它出现得比“毛利转负”早得多。在临界点上，按默认成本路径，美光 FY28–30 毛利率仍约 65%，营收约 $100–140B，净利约 $50–70B；即便 SCA 客户集体违约，毛利率也约 60%，外加 $10–30B 的一次性押金没收。换句话说，保底价的意义不在于防止亏损，而在于防止“从极高利润回到正常利润”的那一段。
  • It arrives long before margins turn negative. At the threshold, on the default cost path, Micron's FY28–30 gross margin is still about 65%, with revenue of about $100–140B and net income of about $50–70B. Even if SCA customers default en masse, gross margin is about 60%, plus a one-off $10–30B of forfeited deposits. The floors' job is not to prevent losses but to slow the slide from extraordinary profit back to normal profit.
  • 更可能的结局是重谈，而不是违约。交易对手多为现金充裕的云厂与 OEM，真正的风险是“用更长的期限换更低的保底价”。RBC 的说法是：SCA 在周期下行中的可执行性“尚未经过检验”。[40]
  • Renegotiation is likelier than default. The counterparties are mostly cash-rich clouds and OEMs, so the realistic risk is a lower floor traded for a longer term. RBC puts it this way: the enforceability of SCAs in a cyclical downturn "has yet to be tested".[40]
  • “量违约”比“价违约”更危险。若客户根本用不上这批货（AI 资本开支收缩），利用率下降会让临界点大幅变浅：利用率 60% 时，即便价格只跌到 0.45 倍以下一点，违约也可能划算。
  • Volume default is more dangerous than price default. If a customer simply cannot use the goods (an AI capex pullback), a lower use value makes the threshold far shallower: at 60%, default can pay as soon as prices dip modestly below the floor.

10 / WAFER INTENSITY

为什么 HBM4/4E 更“吃”晶圆：五个相乘的损耗Why HBM4/4E eats more wafer: five losses that multiply

业内用“交换比”（trade ratio）描述 HBM 的晶圆消耗：在同一制程下，生产 1GB HBM 需要的晶圆面积是 1GB DDR5 的几倍。美光是唯一公开给出数字的厂商：约 3 倍，而且“只会随代际增加”；在 2026 年 Hot Chips 上，美光 HBM 设计院士说这个差距“肯定不会变好”。[28,44] 这个倍数不是一个原因造成的，而是五个因素相乘。

The industry describes HBM's wafer appetite with the "trade ratio": on the same node, how many times more wafer area 1GB of HBM needs than 1GB of DDR5. Micron is the only vendor to publish a number, about 3x, and says it "only increases with future generations"; at Hot Chips 2026 its HBM design fellow said the gap is "definitely not getting better".[28,44] The multiple has no single cause; five factors multiply.

A Samsung memory executive said on Oct 1 that HBM's share of global DRAM wafer capacity will rise from about 20% to "close to 30%" next year; Samsung has also said HBM will take more than a third of global DRAM wafer capacity in 2027.[52] A quick calculation: if HBM's wafer share rises from 22% to 30% in 2027, the wafer share left for conventional DRAM drops from 78% to 70%, about 10% less; for conventional bits still to grow 15% or more, total wafer capacity and bits per wafer must rise about 30% combined (inference). That is why HBM's success tightens conventional DRAM, and why Micron says HBM's growth "further pressures non-HBM supply".[2]

11 / PER-WAFER ECONOMICS

每片晶圆的账：HBM 已经不是利润冠军Per-wafer economics: HBM is no longer the profit champion

交换比的直接后果是：HBM 的每 Gb 售价必须是 DDR5 的“交换比倍数 + 封装成本”以上，才能在每片晶圆上赚得一样多。2026 年常规 DRAM 暴涨、HBM 价格却在 2025 年就锁定，这个关系被打破了。

The direct consequence of the trade ratio is that HBM must sell for at least "trade ratio × DDR5 price plus packaging" per Gb to earn as much per wafer. With conventional DRAM soaring in 2026 while HBM prices were locked in 2025, that relationship broke.

  • TrendForce：自 1Q26 起，HBM 每片晶圆的营收和利润都已低于 DDR5 64GB RDIMM。[51]
  • TrendForce: since 1Q26, HBM has earned less revenue and profit per wafer than DDR5 64GB RDIMM.[51]
  • 伯恩斯坦（6 月）：按年中价格，常规 DRAM 每片晶圆营收是 HBM 的 2 倍以上、毛利约 3 倍；即便 2027 年 HBM 涨 2–2.5 倍，也不足以完全弥合。[53] 10/6 又说 2027 年 HBM 将“比常规 DRAM 更不赚钱”。[38]
  • Bernstein (June): at mid-2026 prices conventional DRAM earns more than 2x the revenue and about 3x the gross profit per wafer of HBM, and even a 2–2.5x HBM price rise in 2027 would not fully close the gap.[53] On Oct 6 it added that HBM will be "less profitable than conventional DRAM in 2027".[38]
  • 美光：HBM 的盈利能力已“落后于”常规 DRAM，2027 年价格将“高得多”，以缩小毛利差距。[52,3]
  • Micron: HBM profitability had "fallen behind" conventional DRAM, and 2027 prices will be "far higher" to narrow the margin gap.[52,3]

用模型 5 的默认值（HBM4 交换比约 3.4、DDR5 合约价约 $2.2/Gb 为我们的估计）：2026 年 HBM3E（$1.5/Gb）每片晶圆营收只有 DDR5 的约 21%，HBM4（$2.08/Gb）约 25%；2027 年 HBM4 涨到约 $4.51/Gb 后升到约 58%。要打平，要么 HBM 涨到约 $7.6/Gb，要么 DDR5 合约价跌到约 $1.3/Gb（跌约 42%）。

With Model 5's defaults (HBM4 trade ratio about 3.4; DDR5 contract about $2.2/Gb, our estimate): in 2026 HBM3E at $1.5/Gb earned only about 21% of DDR5's revenue per wafer and HBM4 at $2.08/Gb about 25%; at about $4.51/Gb in 2027, HBM4 rises to about 58%. Parity needs HBM at about $7.6/Gb, or DDR5 contract prices down to about $1.3/Gb (a fall of about 42%).

含义：HBM 从“股票”变成了“债券”Implication: HBM has turned from equity into a bond

HBM 现在是“长约、年度定价、波动小、每片晶圆利润偏低”的业务，常规 DRAM 反而是高弹性、高利润的部分。这有三个推论：① 只要 DDR5 价格维持高位，厂商没有动力过度扩张 HBM，这在支撑 2027–28 年 HBM 价格；② 一旦常规 DRAM 进入下行，HBM 的年度合同价和每片晶圆对比会重新占优，厂商会把晶圆转回 HBM，届时 HBM 价格承压（高盛提示 2028 年 HBM 价格压力）；③ 三星把 HBM 晶圆占比推到 30–40%，本质上是在“锁量”，而不是“追利润”。[54]

HBM is now the long-contract, annually priced, low-volatility business with lower profit per wafer, while conventional DRAM is the high-beta, high-margin part. Three consequences follow: (1) while DDR5 prices stay high, makers have no incentive to overbuild HBM, which supports 2027–28 HBM pricing; (2) once conventional DRAM turns down, HBM's annual contract prices and per-wafer comparison regain the upper hand, wafers shift back to HBM, and HBM prices come under pressure (Goldman flags 2028 HBM pricing pressure); (3) Samsung's push to 30–40% of wafers on HBM is about locking in volume, not chasing margin.[54]

12 / CUSTOM BASE DIE

定制 base die（NVHBM）：毛利率机械下降约 2 点，真正的风险在议价权Custom base die (NVHBM): about 2 points of mechanical margin dilution; the real risk is bargaining power

“定制 HBM”是把客户专属的逻辑放进堆叠最底层的 base die：主要是内存控制器和定制 PHY（物理层）或芯片间互连，也可能加缓存或近存计算。为此，base die 必须从 DRAM 工艺换成逻辑工艺（台积电 N12/N5/N3P 或三星 SF4/SF2）。英伟达 2026 年 8 月发布的 NVHBM 最典型：base die 由英伟达设计，内含控制器与专有 PHY，宣称比标准 HBM4E 带宽 +30%、HBM 功耗 −15%，并能为计算芯片腾出最多 25–30% 面积；英伟达把它定义为多家存储厂都能供货的标准实现，亚马逊 Trainium4 是首个客户。[55,56,57]

"Custom HBM" puts customer-specific logic into the base die at the bottom of the stack: mainly the memory controller and a custom PHY or die-to-die link, possibly caches or near-memory compute. That forces the base die from a DRAM process onto a logic process (TSMC N12/N5/N3P or Samsung SF4/SF2). NVIDIA's NVHBM, announced in August 2026, is the clearest case: NVIDIA designs the base die with the controller and a proprietary PHY, claims +30% bandwidth and −15% HBM power versus standard HBM4E, and frees up to 25–30% more compute-die area. NVIDIA defines it as a standard implementation that several memory makers can supply; Amazon's Trainium4 is the first customer.[55,56,57]

毛利怎么变：取决于 base die 的成本由谁承担How margins move: it depends on who carries the base-die cost

以 2027 年 12 层 48GB HBM4E 为例（标准 ASP 假设 $1,730，即把 2027 年 HBM4 的每 Gb 价格套到 384Gb），定制 base die 比标准品多 $40 成本：

Take a 12-high 48GB HBM4E in 2027 (standard ASP assumed at $1,730, i.e. 2027 HBM4 per-Gb pricing on 384Gb), where the custom base die adds $40 of cost:

  • A 按成本转嫁：毛利额不变，毛利率从 87.2% 降到 85.3%（约 −2.0 点）。毛利率被“稀释”的幅度 = 毛利率 × 增加成本 ÷ (ASP + 增加成本)。
  • A Pass-through at cost: gross profit unchanged, margin 87.2% → 85.3% (about −2.0 pts). Dilution = gross margin × added cost ÷ (ASP + added cost).
  • C 溢价 10%：毛利额 +11%，毛利率只降约 0.7 点。这就是美光说 NVHBM“更赚钱”的唯一途径。
  • C 10% premium: gross profit +11%, margin down only about 0.7 pts. This is the only route to Micron's "more profitable" claim.
  • D 长约内自行消化：毛利额 −2.7%，毛利率 −2.3 点。韩媒指出，HBM 大多走长约，SK 海力士难以把台积电的 base die 成本转嫁出去。[59]
  • D Absorbed under a fixed-price LTA: gross profit −2.7%, margin −2.3 pts. Korean press notes that most HBM sells under long-term agreements, making it hard for SK hynix to pass TSMC's base-die cost on.[59]
  • E 客户自供 base die（寄售）：收入和成本同时剔除 base die，毛利额不变，毛利率反而约 +1.5 点——纯会计效应。
  • E Consignment (customer supplies the base die): it drops out of both revenue and cost; gross profit unchanged, margin about +1.5 pts, a pure accounting effect.
  • 下行期（ASP 减半）：A 的稀释扩大到 −3.3 点，D 扩大到 −4.6 点。
  • In a downturn (ASP halves): dilution widens to −3.3 pts under A and −4.6 pts under D.

base die 外购本身对美光只是 ±$0.5–2B 量级的毛利问题（FY28 HBM 约 $42B、一半为定制品）。更大的变量是价值链的重新分配：设计价值（控制器、PHY）落到英伟达，制造价值的一部分流向台积电，而英伟达定义的多供应商标准让三家的核心芯片堆叠更可替代。HBM 价格每被压低 10%，美光少约 $4.2B 毛利（约合每股 $3）；按 2028 年基准份额，SK 海力士约少 $9.7B、三星约少 $8.3B。反过来，单客户定制件提高双方的转换成本：一代产品内的份额更稳定，但库存不能转卖给别的客户。Wccftech 认为 NVHBM 让英伟达拿走了“大部分 HBM 价值”，这是观点而非数据。[64]

Buying the base die in is, by itself, a gross-profit issue of ±$0.5–2B for Micron (FY28 HBM about $42B, half of it custom). The larger variable is how value gets redistributed: design value (controller, PHY) goes to NVIDIA, part of the manufacturing value to TSMC, and an NVIDIA-defined multi-vendor standard makes the three makers' core-die stacks more interchangeable. Each 10% cut in HBM prices costs Micron about $4.2B of gross profit (about $3 a share); at 2028 base shares, SK hynix would lose about $9.7B and Samsung about $8.3B. In the other direction, single-customer parts raise switching costs for both sides: share within a generation becomes stickier, but inventory cannot be resold to another customer. Wccftech argues NVHBM lets NVIDIA capture "most of the HBM value"; that is opinion, not data.[64]

要点Takeaway

CPU 侧内存是整个机柜里弹性最大的一项：它插在插槽上，可以先少装、以后再加；它的单 CPU 成本（约 $1.9 万）已超过单 GPU 的 HBM（约 $0.5 万，UBS 口径）。[99]最大的买家已经示范：内存一旦超过系统成本的 25–30%，就会从设计上把它“抠”出来。总比特未必减少（同样的供给覆盖了约两倍的机柜数），但这一细分市场的价格弹性在上升。值得注意的反向信号是：郭明錤称英伟达恢复了 Rubin CPX，并从 128GB GDDR7 改为 168GB HBM4（未获证实），这是增加内存的方向。

CPU-side memory is the most elastic line in the rack: it is socketed, so it can be under-populated now and filled later, and per CPU it already costs more (about $19k) than the HBM per GPU (about $5k, per UBS's figures).[99] The largest buyer has shown it will design memory out once it passes 25–30% of system cost. Total bits may not fall (the same supply now covers about twice the racks), but price elasticity in this segment is rising. One counter-signal: Ming-Chi Kuo says NVIDIA revived Rubin CPX and switched it from 128GB of GDDR7 to 168GB of HBM4 (unconfirmed), which adds memory.

(1) In the base case CPU-side DRAM bits grow about 26% / 31% / 24% in 2026–28, faster than industry supply, absorbing about 39% / 56% / 47% of each year's new bits, of which agents contribute about 9 / 19 / 21 points. Together with HBM it is the data center's second engine. (2) But its elasticity sits with the buyer: each extra 10% of GB per server takes about 24 more points of the year's supply increment, while 5 more points of unit growth take only about 10. NVIDIA's SOCAMM cut and the RDIMM downsizing are this lever in action. (3) Take Muse: even with all 4 million users resident at 17.5GB each, that is only about 70PB (about 0.2% of 2026 world DRAM); 100 million users with 10% resident at 8.7GB each need about 87PB (about 0.2% of 2026 world DRAM); 100 million fully resident at 17.5GB need about 1.75EB (about 4%). Excess returns peak in 2026–27. 2028 is the inflection: new capacity (ID1, ID2, M15X, Yongin phase 1, P5, CXMT's ramp) and buyers' accumulated design-outs arrive together. Normalized margins will very likely stay well above the 2019 and 2023 troughs, thanks to HBM long-term contracts, floors and China's segmentation, but well below the 2026 peak. The biggest swing factor is how far buyers cut memory per system once it exceeds 25–30% of system cost. Contracts change the shape of the cycle, not its existence.

No public source splits bits by application. SCA

RPO

照付不议Take-or-pay

NBM / LTA
闪迪的“新商业模式”协议 / 长期供货协议。Sandisk's "new business model" agreements / long-term agreements.
交换比Trade ratio

TSV / KGD / KGSD

Base die
HBM 堆叠最底层的逻辑芯片，负责接口与（定制时）控制器。The logic die at the bottom of an HBM stack, handling the interface and, when custom, the controller.
NVHBM
英伟达设计 base die 的定制 HBM4E 标准实现，多家存储厂可供货。NVIDIA's custom HBM4E implementation with an NVIDIA-designed base die, supplied by several makers.
MR-MUF / TC-NCF

CMBU / CDBU / MCBU / AEBU
美光的云存储 / 核心数据中心 / 移动与客户端 / 汽车与嵌入式事业部。Micron's Cloud Memory / Core Data Center / Mobile & Client / Automotive & Embedded units.
