## What changed

- Samsung guided record third-quarter profit, almost all from memory. TSMC’s September revenue grew 54.6% from a year earlier.
- OpenAI’s annualized revenue came in near $50B, below the figure that had been circulating. Capacity providers fell hardest; chip suppliers less.
- Hard-drive makers kept sliding after Toshiba’s capacity plan.

## Where the bottleneck sits

Still memory. Every data point this week said supply, not demand, sets the pace. Networking and optics remain strong.

## My views

- SK hynix and the memory views: unchanged, catalysts later this month.
- Seagate is down about 8% since the view opened; nothing in the fundamentals has changed, so the view stays.
- Opened a small short on a levered capacity provider as a hedge against an AI-revenue scare.

## Next week

- ASML and TSMC results; the OCP Global Summit for rack, cooling and networking designs.
