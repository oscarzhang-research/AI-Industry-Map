Samsung guided third-quarter operating profit to about **107.4 trillion won**, up 782.5% from a year earlier, on sales of about 195 trillion won. Analysts credit memory for most of it; full results come on October 29.

My take: the read-across for SK hynix (October 27) and Micron (December) is positive. The open question is how much of this is already in 2027 estimates.
