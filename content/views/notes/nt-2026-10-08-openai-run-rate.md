OpenAI told investors it reached roughly $50B of annualized revenue at the end of September, below the $68B figure that had been widely reported (which reportedly included partners’ gross revenue). Oracle fell nearly 6%, CoreWeave nearly 8% and Nvidia 3%.

My take: the market is now sensitive to the gap between lab revenue and the capacity being financed against it. The most levered capacity providers move first, the chip suppliers last.
